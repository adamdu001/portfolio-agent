Interviewer: Can you walk us through some of the more significant portfolio moves you made this quarter?
Portfolio Manager: Sure. We've been reducing Kraft Heinz for a few quarters now. The thesis was always about brand value and pricing power and, while that hasn't completely broken down, we're less convinced about the pace of the recovery. We're still holders, but it's not a top-conviction name for us anymore.
On the new side, we added Qualcomm in January. We think the AI-on-device cycle is being underestimated, and QCOM is one of the clearest beneficiaries, with a strong royalty stream to cushion the wait. We also initiated a position in Boeing. That is a long-horizon recovery play. The regulatory and reputational issues are real, but we think the franchise value is intact and the stock is pricing in a worst case that will not materialise.
We exited Warner Bros. Discovery entirely. The content spend required to compete in streaming is simply higher than we modelled, and the balance sheet does not give the company enough room to manoeuvre.
